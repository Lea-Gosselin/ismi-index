01 / ISMI

Shock momentum

The Inflation Shock Momentum Index (ISMI) measures the share of PCE categories experiencing consecutive months of positive inflation shocks minus the share experiencing consecutive negative shocks. The index provides a real-time measure of inflation momentum across different product categories.

Here, the methodology is applied to US PCE and euro area HICP components.

02 / PERSISTENCE

Time-varying persistence

A state-space model estimates time-varying inflation persistence. The dashed horizontal line represents the estimated long-run persistence parameter.
